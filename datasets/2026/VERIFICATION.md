## Notes

Four things in this season's record deserve a sentence of explanation.

1. The season was still in progress when the data was collected. The dataset holds every game completed through November 21, 2026, which is week 12. Rivalry week (week 13) had not been played, so the 72 games scheduled from November 25 onward, the three conference championship games, and the Army-Navy game are listed under the excluded games above as not played.

2. The Coaches Poll released September 8 lists 26 teams because two teams tied for 25th. The primary source and ESPN list the same 26.

3. Three games needed a new spelling in the alias table before NCAA.com matched them: NCAA.com writes Southern as "Southern U.", UT Rio Grande Valley as "UTRGV", and Mississippi Valley State as "Mississippi Val.". With those aliases every game in the season matches.

4. The AP poll released October 4 is the one poll where College Poll Archive's tallies differ from ours. The archive also swaps Missouri and Tennessee at 14th and 15th. The primary source and ESPN agree with each other, and NCAA.com's published copy of that poll (https://www.ncaa.com/news/football/article/2026-10-04/top-five-holds-alabama-rises-latest-ap-top-25-college-football-rankings) lists Missouri 14th with 755 points and Tennessee 15th with 746, as we do.
